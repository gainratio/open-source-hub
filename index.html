What a verified load checks

  1. 1Who signed it. An Ed25519 key from a pinned trust root, with revoked keys refused.
  2. 2That it is current. A monotonic sequence blocks replayed or rolled-back releases.
  3. 3That it arrived intact. Every chunk is addressed by sha256 and decompressed only after it matches.

What “verified” means here

Not a badge. Three properties you can test in the repository, on the live site, or with the network disconnected.

Signed, not assumed

The publisher signs a pointer; the client pins a public key and refuses a signer it does not trust or has revoked.

Addressed, not trusted

Manifests and chunks are named by their SHA-256. A byte that changed is a load that fails, not a warning in a log.

Local, not harvested

The browser products do the sensitive work on your machine. There is no account, no analytics script, and no upload of the names or birth data you enter.
